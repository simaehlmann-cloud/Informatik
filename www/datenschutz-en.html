2. Principle: no data collection

Bit für Bit does not process any personal data on the provider’s servers. The app works completely offline. There are no accounts with the provider, no registration, no analytics or tracking services, no advertising and no sharing of data with third parties. Profile names and passwords (if profiles are used) stay on the device. This makes the app suitable for use in class.

3. Data stored on the device

Only the following is stored, in the app’s local storage on the device concerned:

  • Profiles: a first name, nickname or code, the password only as a hash (PBKDF2 with salt – the password itself is not stored anywhere), the chosen level and, if applicable, the class joined
  • Learning progress per profile: stars per station and level, number of attempts, mistakes, tips used, practice time and date of last use
  • Parent/teacher area: the password of this area only as a hash; on the teacher’s device also the classes created, the class list (codes) and the collected results
  • settings for use (without profiles/home/school, guest access), language, appearance, text size and sounds

Input in the exercises – such as passwords you try out – is neither stored nor transmitted. The read-aloud feature uses your device’s speech output; the app itself transmits no data. A profile is logged out automatically after 15 minutes without use.

If the app is used “without profiles”, there is no name and no password; only the device’s level and learning progress are stored. When practising “as a guest”, progress is kept only until the app is closed and is then discarded.

None of this data can be viewed by the provider. Profiles and classes can be deleted in the parent or teacher area; classes and collected results are deleted automatically at the end of the school year (31 July). Removing the app deletes everything.

3a. Passing on results by QR code (school)

In school use, a profile can show its learning progress as a QR code. The teacher reads it with the camera of their device. The code only contains the name or code, stars, attempts, mistakes, tips, practice time and timestamps plus a checksum. The transfer takes place directly from screen to camera, without internet and without any server of the provider.

The school is responsible for processing student data on school-owned devices and on the teacher’s device. We recommend using codes instead of full names.

3b. Camera

The camera is only used when you scan a class or hand-in code, and only with your permission. The images are analysed on the device only and are neither stored nor transmitted. Alternatively, a photo can be chosen or the code pasted as text.

4. Purchasing the app

The app is offered as a one-time purchase through the store from which you install it. Payment and processing are handled exclusively by the operator of that store under its own privacy policy. The provider does not receive any information that identifies individual buyers, only aggregated sales figures. The app itself contains no purchase buttons and no in-app purchases.

5. Crash reports and statistics from the store

If you have allowed this in your device settings, the store operator provides the provider with aggregated, non-personal statistics and crash reports. The app has no influence on this; you can change the setting on your device at any time.

6. Contact by email

If you write an email via “Send feedback” or otherwise, your email app opens. Unless you change it, the email contains the app version, the selected language and information about the type of device so that bugs can be traced. Your message and email address are used only to deal with your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and deleted once they are no longer needed for that purpose.

7. Permissions

The app only uses the camera to scan codes (see 3b). Microphone, location and contacts are not used; the app only accesses photos if you choose a photo of a code yourself. The app does not connect to the internet.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection under Art. 15 to 21 GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Since the provider does not store any personal data from the app, requests for access or erasure addressed to the provider will generally have nothing to act on. You can delete all data yourself in the app at any time.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated as soon as app features relevant to data protection change. The current version is always available in the app.
